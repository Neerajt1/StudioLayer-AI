SL-018B – Complete the Look Pipeline Integration
Implementation Guidelines
Preserve the existing architecture and backward compatibility.
Do not redesign the Render Orchestrator or Hybrid Strategy.
Do not modify the existing UI.
Complete the integration of the Complete the Look feature implemented in SL-018A.
At the end of implementation, provide a detailed Engineering Report.
OBJECTIVE
SL-018A successfully implemented the Complete the Look UI and Outfit Completion Engine.
However, the generated outfit specification currently stops at the frontend and is not incorporated into the final rendering prompt.
The objective of this sprint is to complete the pipeline so the selected outfit reaches the PromptComposer and influences the generated render.

Update the rendering request model to support carrying the user's outfit selection.

Complete the Look selection
Generated outfit specification
