PART 3

Prompt Composition

Build a PromptComposer module.

The PromptComposer converts the Outfit Recommendation Object into a natural language rendering prompt.

Example

Uploaded Product

Blue Slim Jeans

↓

Generated Prompt

"A full-body studio fashion photograph of a young adult male wearing the uploaded blue slim-fit denim jeans as the hero product, paired with a premium white crew-neck T-shirt, clean white leather sneakers, and a brown leather belt. Neutral contemporary styling, professional catalogue photography, realistic proportions, accurate garment fit, natural lighting, clean white background."

The PromptComposer must always prioritize

• uploaded garment accuracy
• garment visibility
• catalogue realism

Complementary garments should adapt around the uploaded garment.

------------------------------------------------------------

PART 5

Outfit Composition Rules

The uploaded garment is always the visual focal point.

Complementary garments should be visually neutral.

Avoid highly patterned supporting garments.

Avoid bright colours that distract.

Avoid oversized accessories.

Never hide the uploaded garment.

------------------------------------------------------------

PART 6

Commercial Photography Rules

Every output should resemble a premium fashion catalogue.

Requirements

• Full body

• Natural standing pose

• Neutral expression

• Balanced posture

• Professional lighting

• Clean studio background

• Accurate garment draping

• Realistic footwear placement

• Natural shadows
