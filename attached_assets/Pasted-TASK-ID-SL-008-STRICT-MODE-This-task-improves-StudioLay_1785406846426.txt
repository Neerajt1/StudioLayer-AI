------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 2

Create the StudioLayer AI Identity Standard.

Every identity should follow these specifications:

BACKGROUND

- Pure white or light grey studio background
- No objects
- No scenery
- No furniture

LIGHTING

- Soft, even studio lighting
- No harsh shadows
- No coloured lighting
- Consistent across all identities

POSE

- Standing upright
- Arms naturally away from torso
- Feet visible
- Front-facing
- Neutral stance

CAMERA

- Eye-level
- Full body
- Entire body inside frame
- High resolution
- Minimal perspective distortion

EXPRESSION

- Neutral
- Slight smile only
- No exaggerated emotion

CLOTHING

- Neutral fitted clothing
- Minimal texture
- Plain clothing without patterns
- No jackets
- No accessories
- No handbags
- No scarves
- No hats
- Clothing must not interfere with virtual try-on

IMAGE QUALITY

- Sharp focus
- Studio photography quality
- Catalog-grade
- Consistent framing across all identities
